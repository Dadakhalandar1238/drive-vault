Your files are only ever visible to you.

Signing in only grants access to files this app itself creates, plus a hidden configuration folder Google reserves for the app — never your existing Drive contents. Nothing is stored on our server: your file index and connected-drive list live encrypted inside your own Google Drive, and your login session is a signed, encrypted cookie in your browser. Nobody operating this app can read your files.
